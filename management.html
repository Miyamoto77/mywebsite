Our approach

The details agreed upfront.

Explore an arrangement where Kestel Management rents your property and takes on the management responsibilities set out in the contract.

01 / TERMS

Rent and agreement

Discuss the rent, payment dates, duration and handover. The proposed use and any authority to sublet are reviewed with you before terms are agreed.

02 / MANAGEMENT

Day-to-day responsibilities

Set out who handles occupier communication, property visits, maintenance coordination and issue reporting, with clear approval and spending limits.

03 / PROPERTY CARE

Condition and upkeep

Agree the starting condition, repair responsibilities, communication arrangements and how the property will be handed back at the end of the agreement.

How it works

Start with a conversation about your property.

Tell us what you own and what you would like to achieve. We review the property before proposing an arrangement.

  1. 01

    Share the property details

    Send the location, property type, bedroom count, condition and availability, together with your expectations.

  2. 02

    Review the proposed arrangement

    Discuss suitability, intended use, permissions, rent and the management scope. Agree what further information is needed.

  3. 03

    Agree the terms and handover

    If the arrangement is suitable for both parties, document the responsibilities and agree the next steps before it begins.
