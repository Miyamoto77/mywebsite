Council area prices, rents, growth and estimated gross yields

Estimated gross yield = average monthly private rent × 12 ÷ average house price × 100. This combines two area averages, with different reference months and property mixes. It is a broad screening measure before fees, finance, tax, maintenance and voids. It does not predict the return on an individual property.

How to interpret these figures

Prices are mix-adjusted UK HPI estimates based on registered residential sales, rather than asking prices or a simple average of individual transactions. One-year house price and rent growth use the publishers’ annual percentage changes. Five-year house price growth is calculated from the published UK HPI index for the same calendar month five years earlier; the published index is rounded.

House price and rent data are joined by the exact ONS local authority code. No missing values are estimated. Charts show monthly observations over the latest ten years. Price and rent observations for a month are shown only where that source publishes them.

Gross yields are calculated by Kestrel, including property-type yields. They combine average annualised rent with average sale price for the same council and broad property type, but are not a matched-property yield calculation. A high area yield alone does not establish that a deal is suitable. Check individual comparables, condition, achievable rent and all costs in the Rental Yield Calculator.

Local statistics can fluctuate more than national figures, particularly where transaction numbers are small. Use longer-term trends and the source methodology. Scotland and Northern Ireland are outside this directory’s coverage.
