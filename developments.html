Our focus

Sites and buildings with potential.

Our initial focus is on two types of opportunity. Each needs a clear understanding of the planning position, costs and local demand.

01 / LAND

Land for new homes

Development land and redevelopment sites where the location, access and planning prospects could support a viable residential scheme.

Useful starting information: site address, boundaries, ownership, asking price and planning history.

02 / CONVERSIONS

A new use for existing buildings

Commercial and mixed-use buildings with potential for residential conversion or redevelopment, subject to the necessary checks and approvals.

Useful starting information: current use, floor area, occupancy, asking price and any existing plans.

Our approach

Understand the opportunity before progressing it.

A site's potential is the starting point. The next step is to understand what could realistically be delivered.

  1. 01

    Review the site

    Consider the location, existing use, planning position, access and the main constraints.

  2. 02

    Test the numbers

    Assess likely values, development costs, finance, contingency and the effect of changing assumptions.

  3. 03

    Agree a practical next step

    Identify the further information and professional advice needed to decide whether to progress.
