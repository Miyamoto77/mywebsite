Our services

Three ways we support your project.

Development services for clients with an existing property, a conversion opportunity or land to develop. We start with your goals and agree the scope around your project.

01 / RENOVATIONS

Renovations

Services for clients looking to improve, reconfigure or update an existing property. We consider your plans and the condition of the building, then agree the scope, budget and delivery requirements.

Tell us about the property, the changes you want to make and your intended budget and timescale.

02 / CONVERSIONS

Commercial to residential

Support for clients seeking to convert commercial or mixed-use buildings into residential accommodation, with attention to feasibility, planning requirements, costs and the proposed scheme.

Share the building's current use, floor area, planning position and any available plans.

03 / LAND

Development land

Services for clients exploring development land, from reviewing site potential and planning constraints to appraisal, project requirements and the route forward.

Share the location, site boundaries, ownership position and what you would like to develop.

Our approach

Your goals shape the project.

Whether you are renovating, converting a building or developing land, a clear brief and realistic numbers give the project a sound starting point.

  1. 01

    Understand your brief

    Discuss your goals, property or site, intended scope, budget and timescale.

  2. 02

    Assess the project

    Review feasibility, likely costs, planning considerations, constraints and the information needed to progress.

  3. 03

    Agree the support

    Set out the services, responsibilities and next steps appropriate to your project.
