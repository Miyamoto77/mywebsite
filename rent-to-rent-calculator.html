How the rent-to-rent calculation works

  • Serviced accommodation: annual accommodation revenue = units × available nights per unit × nightly rate × paid occupancy. Net cleaning costs are spread across the average stay. Monthly results are annual totals divided by 12.
  • Room letting / HMO: annual rent = rooms × monthly room rent × 12 × paid occupancy. Occupancy represents collected room-months, so it can reflect voids and non-payment.
  • Operating cash flow: collected revenue minus landlord rent, fixed bills, revenue-based fees, maintenance reserve and occupancy-linked costs. Fixed monthly costs are paid for all 12 months, even during planned closure or at 0% occupancy.
  • Annual cash-on-cash ROI: annual operating cash flow ÷ upfront cash committed × 100. Cash committed includes non-refundable setup costs, refundable deposit and working capital. No ROI is shown when that total is zero.
  • Break-even occupancy: annual fixed costs ÷ the operating contribution at full occupancy. If more than 100% would be required, the entered rate and cost structure cannot break even. Setup recovery is excluded from this operating break-even point.

Use consistent figures, including unrecoverable VAT within the appropriate cost inputs. Tax, VAT calculations, financing, property appreciation, deposit return and exit proceeds are excluded. This is an annual steady-state model: timing of payments, opening delays and seasonal occupancy changes need a separate cash-flow plan.

Use the net cleaning cost field after any cleaning-fee recovery, and keep that fee out of the accommodation nightly rate. Do not enter the same cost in more than one field. Projected returns are estimates, not guarantees.
