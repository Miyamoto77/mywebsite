  • For deals with no purchase price (rent-to-rent, serviced accommodation, lease option), the fee is a flat £4,000.
  • A minimum fee of £4,000 applies to every deal.
  • A £1,000 reservation deposit is payable to unlock full details and 14 days' exclusivity. It is non-refundable once we disclose the property to you, and it counts towards your fee.
  • The balance is due on exchange of contracts (private investors) or completion (corporate investors).
  • All fees are exclusive of VAT, which is added at the prevailing rate where applicable.

The fee is based on the projected figures you accept in the deal pack and does not change with your actual returns. Full terms, including your cancellation rights, are set out in the sourcing agreement provided before you commit.
