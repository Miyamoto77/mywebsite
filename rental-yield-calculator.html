What these results mean

Gross potential yield = monthly rent × 12 ÷ purchase price. Net operating yield = rent collected after voids and running costs ÷ purchase price. Mortgage payments are deducted separately to calculate cash flow. Management and maintenance percentages apply to collected rent.

Results are before personal or corporation tax. Repayment mortgages include principal repayments in cash flow. Acquisition costs, future growth and sale proceeds are excluded here; use the Deal Analyser for a wider appraisal.
